About Pawsitive Rescue

Pawsitive Rescue was founded in 2016 by a small group of animal lovers who wanted to make a difference in the lives of abandoned and neglected pets. What began as a foster-based effort has grown into a thriving nonprofit that has helped over 1,000 animals find loving homes.

Our mission is simple: to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals in need while promoting responsible pet ownership through education and community outreach. Every adoption supports our work to provide medical care, training, and a second chance for animals who have nowhere else to turn.

At Pawsitive Rescue, we believe every paw deserves a home — and every home can be made brighter by the love of a rescue animal.
